Our Story & Heritage

Considered Living,
Beautifully Made.

Furniture with intention, soul, and enduring craft.

At Fair Home Decor, we believe a home is more than four walls — it is a sanctuary built from pieces that carry warmth, texture, and character. We partner directly with master woodworkers to bring handcrafted, solid wood furniture and curated decor to discerning homeowners and interior designers across the United States.

Rooted in East Brunswick,
Crafted for America

Headquartered at 1 Auer Ct in East Brunswick, New Jersey, Fair Home Decor was born from a desire to push back against the tide of disposable, mass-manufactured flatpack furniture.

We curate and engineer solid wood accent cabinets, bookshelves, console tables, bedside units, and hand-carved photo frames built using time-honored joinery techniques. Each grain pattern is unique, each finish is hand-applied, and every piece is crafted to grow more beautiful with age.

From quiet coastal cottages to contemporary city apartments, our pieces are designed to anchor living spaces with grounding, timeless appeal.

Our Principles

What Guides Every Piece

01

100% Solid Natural Wood

We work exclusively with authentic, sustainably harvested hardwoods. No paper veneers, no synthetic substitutes — only the honest beauty and structural integrity of real wood.

02

Artisan Heritage

Our workshop partners carry generations of woodworking mastery. From dovetail drawers to turned spindle legs, human hands shape and inspect every detail.

03

Nationwide USA Care

Whether you're furnishing a single room or completing a whole-home project, our East Brunswick team oversees nationwide delivery with personalized support.
