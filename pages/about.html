Our Story & Heritage

Considered Living,
Beautifully Made.

Furniture with intention, soul, and enduring craft.

At Fair Home Decor, we believe a home is more than four walls — it is a sanctuary built from pieces that carry warmth, texture, and character. We own and operate our dedicated factory where solid wood furniture and artisanal crafts are manufactured by skilled workers and generational woodcrafters. Guided directly by our USA head office in East Brunswick, New Jersey, and supported by regional storage units across multiple states, we deliver timeless heirloom pieces directly into American homes — with official retail stores opening soon.

Rooted in East Brunswick,
Crafted for America

Our USA Head Office: Fair Home Decor INC. is centrally headquartered at 1 Auer Ct, East Brunswick, NJ 08816, USA. From our New Jersey office, our leadership team directs design, material sourcing, client care, and nationwide logistics.

Our Own Factory & Skilled Workers: Unlike conventional furniture brands that outsource to anonymous third parties, we manufacture our furniture and home crafts in our very own factory. Every solid wood accent cabinet, bookshelf, console desk, brass candelabra, and holiday centrepiece is shaped, carved, and assembled by skilled workers using time-tested joinery techniques. Human touch and artisan pride guide every cut, facet, and hand-rubbed finish.

Strategic Storage Units & Stores Opening Soon: To ensure prompt and seamless nationwide delivery, we operate storage units and fulfillment hubs in different places across the USA. While our comprehensive collection is currently available to explore and order online, we are actively preparing to open physical retail stores and design showrooms in multiple locations very soon!
